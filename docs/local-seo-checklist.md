## 1. Google Business Profile (most important)

1. Go to **business.google.com** and choose **Add your business**, using the Google account that owns Search Console.
2. **Name:** `Flying Advisors`.
3. **Category:** search for and pick the closest match Google offers for a charter broker. Likely candidates are an aircraft charter or charter flight service. Use only categories that appear in Google's list, and pick one primary.
4. **Location:**
   - If clients can visit the Noida office during set hours, add the address.
   - If clients never visit, set it up as a **service-area business** and hide the address. Google requires this for businesses that serve customers elsewhere.
5. **Service area:** add the regions you serve. Google allows up to 20. Example: Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, India, United Arab Emirates, United Kingdom, plus others you really serve.
6. **Phone:** +91 93543 73625. **Website:** https://flyingadvisors.com/
7. **Verify.** Google usually asks for a short video showing the signage, office and business documents, sometimes a postcard or phone call. Have these ready:
   - visible business signage at A-143, Sector 63
   - business registration or GST document
   - proof of the office, such as a utility bill or lease
8. **Hours:** enter the office's real hours. The 24/7 WhatsApp desk can go in the description, not as "Open 24 hours", unless the office really is.
9. **Services:** add each service with a one-line description, copied from the website: Private jet charter, Empty leg flights, Group & event charter, Air ambulance & medical repatriation, Aircraft acquisition, Aircraft sales.
10. **Photos:** logo (`public/assets/brand/fa-logo@2x.png`), cover image (`public/assets/share/og-home-v1.jpg`), the office exterior and interior, and the team. Only use aircraft photos you have the rights to.
11. **Description:** use the short description above, and you may add the broker disclosure.
12. **After verification:**
    - Ask real clients for Google reviews.
    - Never offer incentives, and never write or buy reviews.
    - Reply to every review.

Once live, send the profile link so it can be added to the website's structured data (`sameAs`) and the contact page.

## 4. Things to avoid

- Don't create listings at addresses you don't operate from, or virtual-office addresses, for Google Business Profile.
- Don't create duplicate profiles for the same office.
- Don't list ratings, awards or certifications that can't be shown.
- Don't use different phone numbers on different directories.
